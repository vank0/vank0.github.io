Aura UI concept · v0.2

Popup assistant

An assistant that waits in the corner until you ask.

Hover the bubble, click the sparkles, or hold Space and just say it. Everything on this page is a prop. The concept is the thing in the bottom-right corner.

Try it

  1. Hover the bubble

    It grows upward and deals out four starter prompts. Clicking one sends it straight away, without stopping in the text field.

  2. Click anywhere

    The bubble leaves and stays gone until you reload the page.

  3. Click the sparkles

    The chat grows out of the button with the cursor already in the text field.

  4. Hold Space

    The ring fills for a moment, then it listens. Let go and the answer comes back in the same bubble, read aloud. With the chat open, it lands in the chat instead.

  5. Tap Space

    A short tap is ignored and scrolls the page as usual, so an accidental press never opens anything.

  6. Pick an expert, a model, an effort

    The expert in the chat header changes the starter prompts and the voice. One menu holds the models, grouped by provider, and the effort: model changes how much it says, effort how long it thinks.

  7. Type /

    A slash at the start of a message lists the skills. Arrow keys choose, Enter completes, and the skill stays highlighted in the field. The Skills button does the same.

Design notes

Quiet at rest

One slow ring and a faint glow. Colour floods in only when something changes: a hold, a voice, a thought.

The delay is the loader

The wait that filters out accidental presses is drawn as a ring filling up, so it reads as progress and tells you when to start talking.

Thinking has a shape

The orb shakes like the toy it borrows from, a line of text shimmers through its steps, then words settle in one at a time.

Blue carries, orange speaks

Brand blue holds the surfaces and the actions. The orange-to-yellow AI gradient is kept for the assistant itself: its sparkles and the hold ring.

Responses are simulated. Colours, gradients and type come from the brand theme tokens.
